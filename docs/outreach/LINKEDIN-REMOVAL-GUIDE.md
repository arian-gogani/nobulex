# How to Get Your Old LinkedIn Removed

**Why this is urgent:** Piotr found the Harvard LinkedIn within days of getting serious.
Danielle will find it before your call. Fix this before you send Piotr's email.

## Option 1: Password recovery (try this first, 10 minutes)

1. Go to linkedin.com/login
2. Click "Forgot password?"
3. Enter the email you used when you made the account (whatever you had at the time)
4. If you still have access to that email, you'll get a reset link
5. Reset, log in, go to Settings > Account Preferences > Close Account

## Option 2: LinkedIn identity verification (no email needed, 3-5 days)

If you can't access the original email:

1. Go to: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1379064
2. Click "Get help signing in" or go to LinkedIn Support
3. File a support request
4. They will ask for a government-issued ID (passport works)
5. They verify your identity and give you access
6. Then you close the account

Timeline: 3-5 business days. Do this TODAY so it's resolved before the Danielle call.

## Option 3: Report as inaccurate/compromised (fastest for takedown)

1. While logged into your REAL account (or without being logged in):
2. Go to the old profile URL
3. Click the ... (more) button on the profile
4. Click "Report/Block"
5. Select "Inaccurate information" or "This account may be compromised"
6. Add a note that this is an old account you created as a minor and lost access to

LinkedIn takes minor-created profiles seriously. This can trigger a faster review.
