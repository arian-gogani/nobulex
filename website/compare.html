Why the difference matters

Guardrails filter what goes in and out of a model. They don't prove what happened between input and output. Observability tools show you dashboards. Dashboards are written by the vendor and can't be independently verified.

Receipts are different. A Nobulex receipt is a self-contained proof artifact. It carries the action, the arguments, the result, the rules that were in effect, and a cryptographic signature. Anyone with the public key can verify it. No vendor access needed. No dashboard login. No trust required.

vs ScopeBlind specifically

ScopeBlind builds a product. Nobulex builds a protocol. Both sign receipts with Ed25519. The differences:

Nobulex receipts carry the full covenant (the rules in effect at execution time), not just a policy digest. This means you can answer "what was allowed" and "what happened" from a single artifact.

Nobulex cross-agent handshake requires both agents to verify each other before transacting. ScopeBlind receipts are one-sided.

Nobulex is MIT licensed with no patents. ScopeBlind has 5 Australian provisional patents and uses FSL licensing for the platform.

Nobulex is positioned as the neutral protocol that frameworks adopt (currently in the LangChain governance RFC #35691). ScopeBlind is positioned as a managed product with hosted infrastructure.

Both approaches have value. The question is whether your compliance team wants a vendor or a verifiable standard.
