Common questions

Is the open source version limited?
No. The full protocol ships under MIT. Covenant DSL, enforcement middleware, hash chain, Ed25519 signing, cross-agent handshake, verification CLI. Nothing is held back. Pro and Enterprise add hosted infrastructure on top.
What happens after beta?
Pro pricing will be based on receipt volume. Beta users get locked-in pricing when we launch. The open source version stays free permanently.
Can I verify receipts without your servers?
Yes. Every receipt is independently verifiable offline using just the public key. That's the whole point. You never need to trust us to verify what an agent did.
Do you support EU AI Act compliance?
The protocol satisfies Article 12 (record-keeping) and Article 14 (human oversight) requirements for high-risk AI systems. We have a full compliance mapping document available.
